## The short answer

**1. 1 lakh users a day by 31 December can't be reached. Stop measuring yourself against it.**
Today TestoZa gets 40 to 60 answer sheets a day and about 18 people a day on its marketing pages. 1 lakh a day is roughly 2,000 times more, in 8 weeks. A startup that grows 10% every week is called exceptional. You would need about 160% growth every week, eight weeks in a row. Chasing that number will push you towards vanity work and burn you out.

**2. Your own plan's goal was ₹1 lakh of revenue a month, not 1 lakh users.**
The 90-day plan says: 100 paying customers × ₹999 = ₹1 lakh a month. Somewhere the goal changed from *paying educators* to *users*. On 31 December your family will ask whether this is a business. "Users" doesn't answer that. "People use it every week, more every week, and some pay" does.

**3. The product is working. The distribution and the money plan are not.**
That's good news. It means the hard part is done.

**4. For the next 54 days: two engines, one money line. Pause everything else.**

## The seven mistakes, and the fix for each

| # | Mistake | The evidence | The fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The goal is users, not paying educators | Revenue ₹0. "Free until 31 Dec" means ₹0 on the day of the family review | Sell a **Founding 2027** plan before 31 December (file 03) |
| 2 | Eight channels at once, alone | LinkedIn: 1 visit in 30 days. YouTube: 15 videos, ~805 views, 2 visits. 40+ guides: about 10 educators ever | Keep the 2 channels that bring educators. Pause the rest until 31 Dec |
| 3 | Building faster than distributing | More than 50 guides, videos and features in 30 days, but new educators stayed at ~25 a week | Build only what feeds the two engines or the money line |
| 4 | Candidates are ignored | 80% of visitors arrive on an exam link. "Made with TestoZa" links brought 1 visit a week | An invite card on result pages + a referral reward |
| 5 | No reason to act now | Referrals live since 27 Oct: 0 used. Free with no date gives nobody a push | The deadline everywhere + a 2027 reward for inviting educators |
| 6 | Prices too low to ever pay the bills | At ₹799 a year you'd need about 1,500 yearly customers for ₹1 lakh a month | Price by answer sheets; about 300 paying customers = ₹1 lakh a month (file 03) |
| 7 | Not ready for success | Cloud free-trial credit, Cloudflare's 100,000 requests a day (one 350-candidate exam used ~25,000 in an hour), backups unclear | Pay for the three plans this week, about ₹3,000 a month in total (file 02) |
