## 6. Money

- **Revenue: ₹0.** Everything is free (the "unlock all premium" switch is on). 4 accounts are marked premium.
- **Paid plans today:** ₹49 a week, ₹149 a month, ₹799 a year.
- **What ₹1 lakh a month needs at today's prices:** 1,500 yearly customers, or 670 monthly ones.
- **Costs:** the cloud bill is mostly AI import (about 92% of it in October). Your Google Cloud free-trial credit was about ₹4,760 on 3 November. Google pauses a free-trial project when the credit runs out, unless the account has been upgraded to a paid one.

## 7. The 1 lakh a day arithmetic

- 1 lakh a day is 7 lakh answer sheets a week.
- Today's average educator who runs exams produces about 20 answer sheets a week.
- 7,00,000 ÷ 20 = **35,000 educators running an exam every week.** You have 17 to 23.
- Or about **300 large institutes**, each testing 2,000+ candidates every week. That needs someone selling to institutes and servers built for 10,000 candidates at the same moment.

**How long it takes, honestly:**

| From | Weekly growth | Reaches 1 lakh a day |
|---|---|---|
| 1,500 answer sheets/week (Jan 2027) | 5% a week (good) | mid-2029 |
| 1,500 answer sheets/week (Jan 2027) | 7% a week (very good) | late 2028 |
| 1,500 answer sheets/week (Jan 2027) | 10% a week (exceptional) | around spring 2028 |

(Paul Graham, Y Combinator: "A good growth rate during YC is 5-7% a week. If you can hit 10% a week you're doing exceptionally well.")

## 8. What this means

1. **TestoZa has pull.** Educators in 7 languages and 6+ countries found it without ads, ran exams and came back. That's rarer than you think.
2. **Growth today comes from two places:** ChatGPT and the homepage bring educators, and educators bring candidates. Everything else is noise for now.
3. **The leaks are clear:** 55% of educators who build a test never get a candidate; 80% of visitors (candidates) leave without anyone asking "do you teach too?"; nobody can pay you.
4. **The risks are clear too:** a few big educators make most of the volume, the infrastructure has free-plan limits, and the deadline is 54 days away.
