# 03 · "Free until 31 December": how the big ones did it, and what TestoZa copies

## What a "free until" offer is for

1. **Free** brings people in.
2. **A date** makes them act now instead of "next month".
3. **A founding price** turns some of them into paying customers *before* the date.

Most small companies do the first, half-do the second and forget the third. The third is the one your family will see on 31 December.

---

## How the big ones did it

### 1. Reliance Jio, 2016 to 2017 (the Indian textbook case)

- Free calls and data from September 2016, **"until 31 December 2016"**.
- **Extended once, early, with a new name and a reason**: the "Happy New Year Offer", free until 31 March 2017.
- **Before free ended, existing users were asked to pay once to stay founding members**: ₹99 for "Jio Prime", which kept the best benefits for a year.
- Jio reported **100 million users in about 170 days**, and reportedly about 72 million people paid for Prime by the deadline.

**TestoZa copies:** a hard date, a founding membership people buy *before* the date, and extending at most once, openly.
**TestoZa can't copy:** Jio had shops and phones in every town. Your "shops" are educators. Each one brings 20 to 300 candidates.

### 2. ChatGPT Go in India (Nov 2025), Airtel with Perplexity Pro (2025), Jio with Google Gemini (2025)

Free for 12 to 18 months, to build a habit before charging. Each worked because a partner with millions of customers handed it out.

**TestoZa copies:** free is for building a habit, so measure the habit (exams every week), not sign-ups.

### 3. Dropbox, 2008 to 2010: the two-sided referral

Invite a friend and both of you get extra space. Dropbox went from about 100,000 to about 4 million users in 15 months, and its founder said referrals permanently raised sign-ups by 60%.

**TestoZa copies:** "Invite an educator. When they run an exam with 10+ candidates, you both get a month free in 2027." It costs nothing today, because today is already free.

### 4. Hotmail (1996), Typeform, Calendly: the product advertises itself

Every Hotmail email ended with "Get your free email at Hotmail". Every Typeform form says "Made with Typeform". Every Calendly link shows Calendly to the person booking.

**TestoZa copies:** every result page is seen by a candidate and often a parent. One small, honest line there ("Do you teach? Make a test like this one.") is your Hotmail footer. **Never on the exam screen.**

### 5. Zoom, 2020: free for the people who spread it, paid by organisations

Zoom lifted the 40-minute limit for schools. Teachers adopted it at home; organisations paid.

**TestoZa copies:** a free plan for individual educators stays; institutes pay. Your new pricing page already says this.

### 6. Every good launch offer: a real countdown, a real reason, calm reminders

No timers that reset when you reload. No "only 3 spots left". The new /pricing page counts real days in Indian time and switches itself off on 1 January.

## The honesty rules

- **The date is real.** If you extend, do it once, by 15 December, with a reason ("exam season"), the way Jio did.
- **Every claim is true today.** The new pricing page dropped four claims that weren't: auto-renewing subscriptions, a custom domain, 24/7 support and quizzes from video.
- **Nobody is charged without choosing to pay.**
- **What happens on 1 January is written down before anyone pays** (it's on /pricing now).

## Founding 2027 (the money line)

### The offer

> **Founding 2027.** Pay once before 31 December. Get every paid feature for all of 2027 at a founding price, and keep that price in 2028.

**Who it's for:** coaching institutes, schools and trainers who already ran 2+ exams. Half of the educators who ever had a real exam ran another one, so this group grows every week.

### A suggested 2027 price list (a starting point to test, not a final answer)

Today's prices (₹49 a week, ₹149 a month, ₹799 a year) are too low, and they charge by *time* while both your costs and an institute's value grow with *candidates*.

| Plan | For | Price | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | One educator, one or two batches | ₹0 | Up to 100 answer sheets a month, 5 AI imports a month |
| **Educator Pro** | Tutor or teacher with several batches | ₹199 a month or ₹1,999 a year | More answer sheets and AI imports, your logo |
| **Institute** | Coaching institute or school | ₹999 a month or ₹9,999 a year | Several educators, high limits, logo, priority help |

Why 100 a month on Free: an average educator who runs exams gets about 20 answer sheets a week, so most single-batch educators stay free and the busy ones pay.

**Founding prices (only until 31 December 2026):**

| Founding plan | Price for all of 2027 | Saving |
|---|---|---|
| Founding Educator | ₹999 once | Half the yearly price |
| Founding Institute | ₹3,999 once | 60% off the yearly price |

**Why an institute says yes:** printing a 4-page paper for 200 candidates costs about ₹800 every time, before anyone checks a single sheet. Four exams a month is about ₹3,200 of paper alone.

**The road back to your original goal:** 50 institutes × ₹999 + 250 educators × ₹199 = **₹99,700 a month.** About 300 paying customers, not 1 lakh users.

**How to test the price without talking to anyone:** put the offer up on 17 November. If nobody buys in 10 days, lower the founding institute price once (for example to ₹2,499) and say why. The market answers for you.

### How it works with today's code

1. Admin → Pricing → new plan, for example "Founding 2027 · Institute", with **duration 420 days**. Bought on any day from 7 November, 420 days lasts until at least 31 December 2027.
2. **Add the plan only together with its card on /pricing.** Today's pricing page lists every active plan as "₹0 till 31 Dec", so a founding plan added on its own would show there by mistake. Tell Claude the price and both go in together.
3. Before announcing, make one real payment yourself to prove Razorpay is in live mode end to end. Ask Claude to set up the test so no ₹1 plan ever shows on the public page.

## What not to do

- **Don't run ads saying "free".** Ads for free things bring freebie hunters and students, not institutes.
- **No fake urgency.** No resetting timers, no "3 spots left".
- **Don't extend more than once.**
- **Never write "free forever" or "lifetime free" again.** Write "a free plan stays".
