The October distribution report found that **31 teachers built a test on TestoZa and not one ever had 10 students take it.** Social media can't fix a product gap, but it *can* bring in teachers at a moment when the send-to-students flow now works. So this calendar is judged on one number:

## Brand Context

- **Brand:** TestoZa — testoza.com
- **Audience (buyer):** Owner-teachers of Tier-2/3 coaching institutes (100–500 students), Govt-exam (SSC, Railway, Banking, State) and Science-entrance (JEE, NEET, CUET) segments. Sole decision-maker, budget-careful, no IT staff, lives on WhatsApp. Secondary: YouTube/Telegram tutors.
- **What they fear:** a test link breaking in front of 40 students and their parents. Reputation is the business. Downside of a broken test = humiliation; upside of a working one = convenience. **Every post should lower the perceived risk of the first real exam.**
- **The one fact they need and never hear:** *students don't need accounts* — they enter a join code and their name.
- **Voice:** a fellow teacher who also writes code. Plain, specific, a bit blunt, never salesy. Hinglish (Roman script) on Instagram/YouTube; clear English on LinkedIn. Show, don't claim — a 30-second screen recording beats any adjective.
- **Words to use:** paper, batch, weekly test, join code, result, parents, marks. **Avoid:** "revolutionary", "AI-powered platform", "seamless", "creator economy", "badges".
- **Primary goal:** teachers conduct a first real exam. **Secondary:** a founder-led LinkedIn presence that institute directors and partners can trust.

What actually stops cheating in online exams — and what's theatre.

Theatre:
✗ "AI proctoring" that claims to catch everything
✗ Blocking right-click
✗ Threatening messages on the instruction page

What actually helps:
✓ Full-screen + tab-switch tracking that the student *can see* is on. Most cheating is opportunistic; visible tracking stops most of it.

✓ Question variety across batches, so a screenshot from the 9 AM batch is useless at 5 PM.
✓ Holding results until everyone's done, so answers don't leak mid-exam.
✓ For high stakes: a supervised room. No software replaces a teacher walking the aisle.

Honest truth: no browser can stop a second phone. Design the exam so a second phone doesn't help much.

Full breakdown in the comments.
