## 1 Customer Segments

**BEACHHEAD (B2B - Offline Coaching Centres):**
- Tier-2 / Tier-3 Independent Coaching Institutes (100–500 students)
- Segment A: Govt Exams (SSC, RRB, Banking, State PSC/Police).
- Segment B: Science & National Entrance (JEE, NEET, CUET) — NTA CBT simulation & deep diagnostics.
- Buyer: Owner-Teacher / Director (Sole decision-maker, budget-conscious, zero IT team).
- Addressable Pool: ~25,000 active centres out of 68,000+ registered institutes.

**ADJACENT DIGITAL EDUCATORS (B2B - Low CAC, Self-Serve):**
- YouTube & Telegram Tutors (5k–100k audience) wanting to sell branded CBT test series without paying ₹20k–₹50k upfront for custom apps.

**END-USER / DOWNSTREAM (B2B2C):**
- Tier-2/3/4 Aspirants seeking CBT practice, instant rank analysis, and crash-proof test experience on mobile/desktop.

**FUTURE EXPANSION (Horizon 3):**
- Budget Private K-12 Schools (Class 9–12 Unit tests & board mocks).
- Corporate HR

## 2 Value Propositions

“Auto-Create. Conduct. Analyze. Scale.”

- Any question paper/notes → exam-realistic CBT in <60s (AI Vision)
- 14 hrs of paper work → under 5 minutes
- Authentic NTA interface — students train on the real exam UI
- Secure Anti-Cheat Test Environment.
- Diagnoses WHY marks were lost (4-quadrant matrix), not just the score
- JEE-Adv type partial marking, numerical tolerance, power-cut crash recovery
- 6–7× cheaper than coaching-app incumbents (Classplus, Teachmint)
- Zero setup — runs on a Tier-2/3 tutor's Android phone over 3G/4G
