## Guide: Blooket and Gimkit alternatives (https://testoza.com/blooket-gimkit-alternative)

For teachers who play Blooket or Gimkit in class and want another game, or a score that counts. Written by TestoZa; other products described from their own help pages, checked 5 November 2026.

- **Why teachers switch:** Blooket’s free plan hosts games of up to 60 players (Plus: 300 in most modes; price shown after sign-in); Gimkit Basic is free with any number of players but only its featured modes, about three at a time; Gimkit Pro $59.88 a year or $14.99 a month; groups $650 a year (20 teachers) or $1,000 (school).
- **Why the winner isn’t the best student:** in Blooket’s Gold Quest each right answer opens one of three random chests (gold, multipliers, taking 10% or 25% from a rival, or a dragon that takes yours); in Gimkit’s Classic students earn in-game money and spend it on upgrades, and a wrong answer breaks a streak and can cost money (Insurance softens it). A marked test gives the same score for the same answers.
- **Other games:** Kahoot (speed scoring, up to 1,000 points), Wayground (formerly Quizizz, self-paced), Socrative (percentage, free room of 50), Google Forms.
- **When to use a test instead:** weekly tests for the register, exam mocks (JEE, NEET, SSC, bank, CUET) with negative marking and an NTA-style screen; TestoZa joins by six-digit code with no app or account, AI reads a PDF or photo into the test, rank list and parent notes; free to build and run.
- **Limits:** TestoZa has no game modes, characters, power-ups or podium, and students don’t write the questions.

## Guide: Best proctoring software (https://testoza.com/best-proctoring-software)

Eleven online proctoring tools sorted by the stakes of the exam and the devices candidates own. Written by TestoZa; other products described from their own pages and universities’ pages, checked 5 November 2026.

- **Full suites:** Mercer Mettl (AI and live proctors, dual camera, three-point ID check, secure browser; proctored tests not supported on phones or tablets), Talview (face matching, second camera, automated, recorded or live; the final call stays with the client), Eklavvya (proctoring plus descriptive answer marking; AI proctoring on Enterprise only; per exam credit).
- **LMS proctoring:** ProctorU / Meazure Learning (live human proctors; Oklahoma State lists Live+ $16.80–$32.55 and Record+ $6.30 per exam), Proctorio (browser extension; phones only via a paid mobile add-on), Honorlock (AI with live proctors who pop in, phone detection, leaked-question search), Respondus LockDown Browser and Monitor (no Android; annual licence with 200 Monitor seats).
- **Lighter options:** AutoProctor (proctoring for Google Forms and Microsoft Forms; ₹9 per standard attempt; plans from ₹1,350 a month), Safe Exam Browser (free, open source, Windows, macOS, iPad), TestoZa (browser rules on any phone: full screen, counted tab switches with a limit that can submit, copy and paste off, one device per seat, roll + PIN; no camera), Google Forms locked mode (managed Chromebooks only).
- **Privacy:** DPDP Act 2023 requires verifiable parental consent for under-18s and bars behavioural monitoring of children, with a limited exemption for educational institutions in the 2025 Rules; ask a lawyer before recording minors. AI flags need human review.
- **Limits of TestoZa:** no face match or ID check, no camera, no locked browser, no LMS integration.
