Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a test from a PDF?

Open Generate with AI (app.testoza.com/generate-with-ai) and choose the PDF, or photos of the pages. Press Extract Questions to keep an existing paper's questions, options and answer key exactly, or Generate New Questions to write new ones from a chapter or notes at Easy, Moderate or Tough. Review the questions, set any answer marked orange, and press Save & continue: the test opens in your editable builder. Save Word and PowerPoint files as PDF first.

Can I make a test from a photo of a page?

Yes. Generate with AI reads photos (PNG, JPG or WEBP) as well as PDFs: a textbook page, an old question paper or handwritten notes, including maths, chemistry and Hindi. In the test builder, Fill from photo reads a single question from a picture. TestoZa does not make tests from YouTube videos.

How do I add negative marking to an online test?

Every question in the test builder has a Marks and a Wrong field, for example +4 and −1 for JEE Main and NEET, or +2 and −0.5 for SSC; decimals such as 0.25 or 0.3333 work. Multiple-correct questions can also give partial marks (Proportional or JEE Advanced). TestoZa applies the marking automatically when the paper is submitted.

How do I stop students from cheating in an online test?

TestoZa prevents cheating through multi-layered exam security and proctoring controls. When setting up your test, you can enable full-screen enforcement, tab-switch monitoring, copy-paste disabling, and question and option randomization. If a candidate leaves the exam screen, TestoZa warns them, counts it, and can submit the paper automatically after the number of warnings you choose. There is no camera or microphone proctoring.

Can students take the test on a mobile phone?

Yes, students can take any TestoZa test on mobile phones, tablets, or laptops without installing any apps. Every test link opens smoothly in standard mobile browsers like Chrome, Safari, and Firefox. The responsive interface adapts seamlessly to small screens, maintaining timer visibility, clear question navigation, and full touchscreen support for selecting answers and submitting responses.

How do I share a test with my students?

In My Tests press Conduct exam on the test, then Share link to send it on WhatsApp, or let students scan its QR code. You can also get a 6-digit join code: students type it at testoza.com/join. They enter their name or roll number and start: no app, no account. Step by step: testoza.com/user-guide/quick-start.

Is TestoZa better than Google Forms for conducting tests?

Yes, TestoZa is purpose-built for educational assessments, whereas Google Forms is merely a survey tool. Unlike Google Forms, TestoZa provides built-in exam timers, automatic negative marking, anti-cheating tab-switch detection, randomized question ordering, AI-powered test generation, and comprehensive student analytics with automated rank calculation. It offers a professional computer-based testing experience without requiring complex add-ons.

TestoZa is the best free alternative to Quizizz for educators seeking formal, exam-grade assessments rather than just gamified trivia. TestoZa gives teachers unlimited test creation, AI question generation from PDFs and photos, customizable timers, negative marking, and classroom leaderboards—completely free with zero student limits or paywalled question types.
