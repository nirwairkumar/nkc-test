## 📂 Column-by-Column Fill Guide

Here is the exact data to fill in for each column in the Page 2 matrix, row by row:

### 🗳️ Column 1: Tier 2/3 Small & Medium Test-Prep Coaching Institutes
*   **1. Market Segment Name:** Tier 2/3 Test-Prep Coaching Centers (JEE, NEET, SSC, RRB prep)
*   **2. End User:** Classroom teachers (create/schedule tests) and students (take mock tests on phones/tablets).
*   **3. Task:** Transition from analog paper testing to digital computer-based tests matching national exam formats.
*   **4. Benefit:** Saves 80% test-prep and grading time; mimics exact CBT national portals to reduce student exam-day anxiety.
*   **5. Urgency of Need:** **High** (Painkiller). Centers are losing students to digital platforms because they cannot offer CBT prep.
*   **6. Example End Users:** Tutors running local centers (50-500 students) in Patna, Kota, Madurai, or Nagpur.
*   **7. Lead Customers:** Local coaching networks with 3-5 branches who act as educational authorities in their district.
*   **8. Willingness to Change:** **Moderate**. Highly conservative about tech, but forced to adapt by the national shift to digital exams.
*   **9. Frequency of Buying:** Monthly or annual subscription plan (SaaS model).
*   **10. Concentration of Buyers:** Highly fragmented (hundreds of thousands of independent coaching centers in India).
*   **11. Other Relevant considerations:** Very low budget; no in-house IT support; needs to run on low-end mobile devices.
*   **12. Size of Market (# of end users):** ~500,000+ coaching centers in Tier 2/3/4 India (~50M+ students).
*   **13. Est. value of end user:** ₹12,000 to ₹36,000 per year per coaching institute.
*   **14. Competition/alternatives:** Paper-and-pen testing (status quo); Moodle (complex); Google Forms (basic); expensive LMS.
*   **15. Other components needed for a full solution:** Zero-code test-taking interface; secure proctoring module (tab monitor/wake-lock).
*   **16. Important partners:** Local hardware resellers (tablets/PCs); local prep book publishers.
*   **17. Other relevant personal considerations:** Alignment with founders' mission to help local teachers; easy local physical validation access.

### 🗳️ Column 2: YouTube Educators & Independent Online Tutors
*   **1. Market Segment Name:** YouTube Test-Prep Creators & Online Tutors
*   **2. End User:** YouTube instructors (create tests/notes) and online subscribers/learners (attempt mock tests).
*   **3. Task:** Share mock tests & revision notes in video descriptions; collect student scores and feedback.
*   **4. Benefit:** Increases viewer engagement; easy monetization (token-based or paid test series).
*   **5. Urgency of Need:** **High** (Game-changer). Creators need interactive engagement tools to monetize and retain subscribers.
*   **6. Example End Users:** YouTube educators with 10k-500k subscribers in test-prep categories (e.g., SSC/RRB).
*   **7. Lead Customers:** Tech-savvy YouTube tutors with 100k+ subscribers who sell self-published materials.
*   **8. Willingness to Change:** **Extremely High**. Tech-native, eager to adopt tools that simplify workflows and drive monetization.
*   **9. Frequency of Buying:** Pay-as-you-go AI tokens or monthly Pro plans.
*   **10. Concentration of Buyers:** Fragmented but highly concentrated online (reachable via YouTube, Telegram, Email).
*   **11. Other Relevant considerations:** Virality is extremely high; word-of-mouth recommendations between creators drive growth.
*   **12. Size of Market (# of end users):** ~50,000+ educational content creators / online tutors in India.
*   **13. Est. value of end user:** ₹6,000 to ₹18,000 per year per creator (subscription + AI API credits).
*   **14. Competition/alternatives:** Telegram quiz bots (free but unstructured); hosting custom websites (expensive setup).
*   **15. Other components needed for a full solution:** AI PDF/image-to-test parser; easy links/QR codes for YouTube descriptions.
*   **16. Important partners:** YouTube educational influencers; educational Telegram channels.
*   **17. Other relevant personal considerations:** Maximises founders' skills in AI API scaling; near-zero customer acquisition cost (CAC).

---

### 🗳️ Column 3: Individual Offline Tutors & Private Home Educators
*   **1. Market Segment Name:** Individual Offline Tutors (Home tuition / freelance teachers, 10-50 students)
*   **2. End User:** Solo tutors and private teachers.
*   **3. Task:** Create simple weekly quizzes/tests for small home-tuition batches; review scores; share progress with parents.
*   **4. Benefit:** Eliminates hours spent printing and manual grading; professionalizes their home-tuition service for parents.
*   **5. Urgency of Need:** **Moderate** (Vitamin/Painkiller). Driven by parents demanding digital-ready preparation.
*   **6. Example End Users:** Freelance math/science tutors running evening home classes of 15 students in Tier 2 cities.
*   **7. Lead Customers:** Prominent local private tutors who teach top-performing students in the area.
*   **8. Willingness to Change:** **High**. Eager to use simple, cheap/free mobile tools to reduce administrative workload.
*   **9. Frequency of Buying:** Prefers free tier or low-cost Pro plan (monthly).
*   **10. Concentration of Buyers:** Extremely fragmented (millions of individual tutors across India).
*   **11. Other Relevant considerations:** Highly price-sensitive; requires zero setup time; relies entirely on smartphone interfaces.
*   **12. Size of Market (# of end users):** ~2M+ private tutors in India.
*   **13. Est. value of end user:** ₹2,400 to ₹6,000 per year per tutor.
*   **14. Competition/alternatives:** Paper sheets; oral questions; WhatsApp messages; free basic Google Forms.
*   **15. Other components needed for a full solution:** Mobile-first test creation, automated WhatsApp report card sharing.
*   **16. Important partners:** Parents, local student hubs.
*   **17. Other relevant personal considerations:** Highly aligned with the team's MVP features (which are already fully mobile-friendly).

### 🗳️ Column 4: Budget/Mid-tier Private Schools (Tier 2/3)
*   **1. Market Segment Name:** Budget/Mid-tier Private Schools (Tier 2/3)
*   **2. End User:** School subject teachers (create term exams) and students (take exams in school computer lab).
*   **3. Task:** Digitise school term/unit examinations and compile section-wise grade sheets.
*   **4. Benefit:** Streamlines grading workflow; automates marksheet/analytics generation.
*   **5. Urgency of Need:** **Medium** (Vitamin/Painkiller). Driven by board directives to include digital exam practice.
*   **6. Example End Users:** Teachers at local trust-run private schools (e.g., "DAV Public School", "St. Mary's").
*   **7. Lead Customers:** Local private school associations or prominent district-level academies.
*   **8. Willingness to Change:** **Low to Moderate**. Slow, bureaucratic decision-making; requires approval from school boards.
*   **9. Frequency of Buying:** Annual licensing per school/student database.
*   **10. Concentration of Buyers:** Concentrated (decisions made by school board/principals).
*   **11. Other Relevant considerations:** Requires parent dashboards, high security, and school-wide administrative user roles.
*   **12. Size of Market (# of end users):** ~100,000+ budget/mid-tier private schools in India.
*   **13. Est. value of end user:** ₹24,000 to ₹60,000 per school per year.
*   **14. Competition/alternatives:** Traditional paper printing; enterprise school ERP modules (Fedena, Teachmint).
*   **15. Other components needed for a full solution:** Section-wise marking, grading infrastructure, and admin reporting dashboards.
*   **16. Important partners:** Education board inspectors; school ERP software vendors.
*   **17. Other relevant personal considerations:** Long sales cycle would distract the engineering team from core development in the short term.

### 🗳️ Column 5: Self-Studying Competitive Exam Aspirants (P2P / Self-Practice)
*   **1. Market Segment Name:** Self-Studying Competitive Exam Aspirants (JEE, NEET, Civil Services prep)
*   **2. End User:** Individual students preparing for competitive exams.
*   **3. Task:** Click photos of textbook pages/notes to auto-generate mock tests using AI; practice PYQs; share test cards with study groups.
*   **4. Benefit:** Instantly generates practice tests from reading material; allows targeted weakness analysis; peer comparison in study groups.
*   **5. Urgency of Need:** **Extremely High** (Painkiller). Aspirants struggle to find specific, targeted mock tests for the exact topics they read.
*   **6. Example End Users:** College or high school students preparing for JEE or government exams in their hostels/homes.
*   **7. Lead Customers:** Influential student leaders in exam forums (e.g., Reddit, Quora, Telegram study channels).
*   **8. Willingness to Change:** **Extremely High**. Tech-savvy, constantly trying new AI tools and exam prep apps.
*   **9. Frequency of Buying:** High-frequency token purchases (pay-per-test) or small monthly student subscriptions.
*   **10. Concentration of Buyers:** Highly fragmented (millions of students), but highly active in online communities.
*   **11. Other Relevant considerations:** Highly viral via social sharing (word of mouth is instant); very low brand loyalty; requires gamified UI.
*   **12. Size of Market (# of end users):** 250M+ students in India.
*   **13. Est. value of end user:** ₹500 to ₹1,200 per student per year.
*   **14. Competition/alternatives:** Textbook practice questions; Testbook (paid); mock test booklets.
*   **15. Other components needed for a full solution:** Mobile-camera-to-test OCR integration, Telegram/WhatsApp share buttons.
*   **16. Important partners:** Study YouTubers, coaching peer groups.
*   **17. Other relevant personal considerations:** Matches the co-founders' technical strength in AI-native OCR and document-to-test parsing.
