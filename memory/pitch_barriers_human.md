# Seed Funding Pitch: Human-Aligned Barriers to Entry

**What keeps us ahead?**
It starts with our specialized tech. We’ve built an AI engine that doesn't just "read" text; it actually handles the complex diagrams and messy academic formatting that rural teachers already use. Generic tools simply aren't tuned for this. 

Then there’s the community. By hosting unique, teacher-made materials, we’re building a local network that gets stronger as it grows. Once a teacher’s entire curriculum is on TestoZa, it’s incredibly hard for a generic competitor to pull those students away. 

Finally, I’ve lived through this gap myself as an IIT Madras student from a small town. I understand the "paper-vs-digital" struggle in a way a detached corporate team never will. That firsthand insight is baked into our product, making it resonate with rural educators in a way a big-box solution can't.
