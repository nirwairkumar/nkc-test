### **Other Resources & Capital**

In addition to the seed funding, we leverage the following resources:
1.  **Sweat Equity (Founder's Time)**: 100% of the existing MVP was built by the technical founder independently. We will continue to rely on internal technical expertise for core development.
2.  **Existing Infrastructure**: Core hardware (computers, local networking) used for development and maintenance is already owned by the founder.
3.  **Institutional Mentorship**: As an **IIT Madras** student, the founder has access to the institute's specialized lab resources, entrepreneurship network, and faculty mentorship at no additional cost.
4.  **Community Capital**: We tap into our "Verified Creator" network of early-adopter teachers who provide proprietary content and product feedback in exchange for early access to "Pro" features.
