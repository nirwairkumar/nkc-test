# Customer Validation & Feedback

**Have you tested your idea?**
Yes, we have conducted initial testing with a cohort of students and several local educators in Tier 2 cities to validate our "Zero-Code" mission.

**Key Insights & Feedback:**
The feedback from these sessions was overwhelmingly positive, with educators specifically describing the platform as "uniquely simple and easy to understand" compared to traditional LMS software. A major insight from our teachers was the desire for a more integrated way to share materials publicly and connect with students informally, not just during exams.

**How we incorporated this feedback:**
Based on these insights, we implemented a robust **Social Learning Layer** that allows educators to publish notes and videos alongside their tests. We also refined our test-authoring interface to be even more mobile-friendly, ensuring that a teacher can manage their entire classroom directly from their smartphone. These changes have transitioned TestoZa from a simple testing tool into a comprehensive social community for learning.
