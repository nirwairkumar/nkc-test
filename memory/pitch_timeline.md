# Project Timeline: 9-Week Scaling & Validation Plan

Since 90% of the platform development is already complete, the next 9 weeks will focus on polishing the AI engine and validating the system for nationwide scale.

| Week | What we will do? | Our Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1-2** | **AI Engine Polishing**: Fine-tune the AI to turn complex paper tests into digital ones with near-perfect accuracy. | Reach 99% accuracy in automated test creation. |
| **3** | **Stress Testing**: Test how the system handles thousands of students taking exams at the same time. | Zero lag or crashes during large-scale tests. |
| **4** | **Real-World Validation**: Conduct deep tests with 10 actual coaching centers to see how they use it daily. | Find and fix any final "real-world" usability issues. |
| **5** | **Security & Proctoring**: Final review of anti-cheating and security systems to ensure 100% integrity. | A certified, cheat-proof exam environment. |
| **6** | **Low-Bandwidth Optimization**: Ensure the site works fast on slow 3G internet in remote villages. | Load the entire test in under 2 seconds. |
| **7** | **Pilot Expansion**: Move the 10 testing centers into full operation to prove the business model. | 10 centers successfully running digital mock tests. |
| **8** | **Monetization & Pricing**: Test the "Pro-Plan" and token system to ensure it is affordable for local tutors. | Confirm the pricing model is sustainable for both sides. |
| **9** | **Market-Ready Scale**: Prepare the final plan to expand to 10,000+ coaching centers. | TestoZa 2.0 fully ready for the Indian market. |
