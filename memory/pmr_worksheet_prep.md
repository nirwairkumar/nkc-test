### I. Secondary Market Research Sources and Key Lessons Learned
*(Space provided: ~3 lines per item. Write concisely.)*

*   **A.** 
    *   **Source:** NTA (National Testing Agency) announcements & EdTech industry reports. 
    *   **Key Lesson:** Major exams (JEE, NEET) have shifted entirely to digital CBT formats, creating a massive "Simulated Reality Gap" for rural students preparing on paper.
*   **B.** 
    *   **Source:** Local tier-2 newspaper ads & coaching center marketing. 
    *   **Key Lesson:** Local centers struggle to justify fees against large EdTech giants (like PhysicsWallah) because they lack the digital mock testing platforms to simulate real exams.
*   **C.** 
    *   **Source:** Academic research on digital literacy in Indian education. 
    *   **Key Lesson:** Teachers in Tier 2/3 cities lack access to simple, non-enterprise tools, making a "zero-code" and mobile-first design absolutely essential for adoption.

### II. Targeted Profiles to Engage
*(Space provided: ~2.5 lines for description. Write in short phrases.)*

*   **A. 1st Targeted Profile Name:** Coaching Center Director (Economic Buyer)
    *   **Description:** Runs a 50-500 student institute in Tier 2/3 cities. Focuses on center profitability, student retention, and competing with large online EdTech platforms.
*   **B. 2nd Targeted Profile Name:** Subject Tutor (End User / Test Creator)
    *   **Description:** Non-technical teacher who relies on paper PDFs. Wants to eliminate hours spent manually grading tests and instantly generate digital exams from existing notes.
*   **C. 3rd Targeted Profile Name:** Competitive Exam Aspirant (End User / Tester)
    *   **Description:** High anxiety about digital exam interfaces. Needs a mobile-friendly platform that identically mimics the official NTA portal to practice effectively.
*   **D. 4th Targeted Profile Name:** Parents of Aspirants (Influencer)
    *   **Description:** Wants tangible proof of their child's progress via automated digital report cards and section-wise analytics, justifying the tuition fees.
*   **E. 5th Targeted Profile Name:** Local Hardware Reseller (Potential Partner)
    *   **Description:** Sells low-cost Android tablets to coaching centers. Can bundle TestoZa software to create a full, affordable CBT lab solution for local institutes.
