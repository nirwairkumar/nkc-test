# Storylines

Three different films for the same brief. **A was built** (owner asked for no stops: "choose for the best").

## A. "First Time" — built

- **Device:** a moment in someone's day + life before and after. **Form:** match cuts between the
  paper world and the screen world, bookended by the same exam-hall frame.
- A young SSC aspirant, Priya, sits down at a computer-based exam and freezes: she has only ever
  practised on paper. Her teacher, Verma Sir, prints, invigilates and checks copies at 1:40 a.m.
  The turn comes at dawn; the same classroom moves to phones and the computer lab, results arrive the
  moment they submit, and he eats dinner with his family. Exam day again: the screen is familiar.
- **First two seconds:** the exam hall, cold light, Priya's hand on the mouse, "Time Left 59:58".
- **Beats:** hook (exam hall) → paper world (print, invigilate, night checking, notice board, copying)
  → turn (dawn) → product (AI import, one code, phones, lab, secure) → payoff (results, ranks, nights
  back) → claim (exam hall, calm) → end card + sonic logo.
- **Look:** live action, cool fluorescent paper world against warm daylight digital world; Outfit
  type; white end card with the light-mode wordmark.
- **Sound:** soft female narrator; score moves from sparse A-minor piano to a C-major build and lands
  on the TestoZa signature tune; diegetic sound only in the paper world.
- **Why this one:** the insight is true and urgent for the target (SSC, railways and banking exams are
  computer-based; most coaching mocks are still paper), and it makes the teacher the hero.

## B. "The Red Pen"

- **Device:** one object across the film. **Form:** object-to-object chain.
- The teacher's red pen across years of checking copies; the day he puts it down, his class goes
  online; he uses it only to write the join code on the board. "Some things we keep. Some we let go."
- **Beats:** pen ticks montage → stacks → the last checked copy → pen in the stand → code on the board
  → class on phones → results → pen in his pocket at dinner.

## C. "1:40 a.m., India" (the bold one)

- **Device:** a number lands. **Form:** split screen to one.
- Dozens of teachers in different cities, all awake at 1:40 a.m. checking copies, tiled across the
  frame; one by one their lamps switch off as their institutes move online, until one frame remains:
  a teacher asleep, a phone buzzing with "Results ready".
