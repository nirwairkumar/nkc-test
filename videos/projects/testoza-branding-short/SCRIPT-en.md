# Script — TestoZa Short: Branding Tests (EN)

One `## Lnn` block = one voice clip. Indented lines are spoken. `{cue}` marks the word an on-screen
action must land on (the click/appear moment is pinned just before the word after the cue).
Cues are stripped before text-to-speech.
Voice: Gemini TTS, Puck — male, 22–27, smooth, trustworthy, energetic. No on-screen text in the
picture (owner). Never say "free" (owner rule).

## L01 · Hook
**Shot:** a laptop and a phone, both in the exam, the Apex Academy logo and name on top of each.
**Delivery:** bright, confident hook; a tiny beat before "Here's exactly how".

    {hook}Your institute's logo and name, on every online test. {how}Here's exactly how — step by step.

## L02 · Open branding
**Shot:** the builder: test name typed, scroll to Exam options, tap Institution branding.
**Delivery:** quick and friendly, moving along.

    {title}Name your test, {branding}then open Institution branding.

## L03 · Logo, name, colour, font
**Shot:** the logo drops into the box, "Apex Academy" typed, blue picked, Georgia font.
**Delivery:** energetic, each step punchy; a proud little lift on "That's your brand".

    {logo}Drop in your logo, {name}type your institute's name, {color}pick your colour, {font}and a font. {branded}That's your brand — done.

## L04 · Questions, fast
**Shot:** three questions typed fast, correct answers tapped, Save test.
**Delivery:** fast and fun, riding the speed.

    {q1}Now add your questions — {right1}tap the right answer, next one, {add3}and one more — all in seconds. {save}Then hit Save.

## L05 · Go live
**Shot:** My Tests → Conduct exam → Start conducting → Copy link → Get a join code.

    {conduct}Then Conduct exam, {start}start it, {copy}copy the link — {code}or share a six-digit join code.

## L06 · On a laptop
**Shot:** a laptop: the candidate opens the link, types a name, starts; punch-in on the logo bar.
**Delivery:** build up, then a delighted reveal on "your logo and name".

    {dopen}Students open the link, {cname}type their name, {dgo}start… {dbrand}and boom — your logo and name, right on top.

## L07 · On a phone
**Shot:** a phone: testoza.com/join, six digits, name, Start; the branded exam; answers tapped.

    {typecode}On a phone? They type the code, {pgo}tap Start, {pbrand}and your brand is right there too. {ans1}Every question, under your name.

## L08 · End
**Shot:** TestoZa logo, testoza.com.
**Delivery:** warm, confident close.

    {endcard}Your institute. Your test. {endurl}Make yours today on testoza.com.
