# Script — TestoZa Short: Branding Tests (TA dub)

Tamil audio track for the finished English Short (YouTube multi-language audio). Same `## Lnn` ids and
`{cue}` names as SCRIPT-en.md: capture/dub.mjs places each line so every cue word is spoken just after its
on-screen action. App words stay English, as they appear on screen. Never say "free".

**Delivery:** bright, confident hook; a tiny beat before "Here's exactly how".
