## Line 3 — Test details (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Unhurried; one field at a time, matching the cursor.

    In the menu on the left, click Create Test. At the top, type the test name — JEE Main Mini Mock. Just below it, add a one-line description. Candidates see this before they start.
    Now pick the time. Tap the three-hour button, and it fills in a hundred and eighty minutes for you.
    "Only my students" means the test is visible only to the people you send the link to.
    In Marking, choose JEE / NEET — plus four for a right answer, and minus one for a wrong one.
    And if you open Institution branding, you can add your institute's name and logo too.

## Line 5 — Q1 in Hindi + options (Frame 6)

**Delivery:** A little delight when the words turn into Hindi.

    Now, the same question in Hindi. Above the question, click the button next to English, search for Hindi in the menu, and pick it.
    Now just type in English letters — e, k… p, a, t, t, h, a, r — and the moment you press space, each word turns into Hindi. So right below the English question, you get the same question in Hindi.
    The options need a square root, so open Maths & symbols. This is Sy Pad. Click option A, write the root, and press Insert.
    All four options are in. Now click the letter of the correct answer — D. That's it. One question, two languages.

## Line 6 — Q2 diagram from the paper (Frame 7)

**Delivery:** Tip-sharing tone; slow on the key combo.

    This question has a diagram, and there's no need to redraw it. Click Picture, and choose the screen button. Now, in the PDF, press Windows, Shift and S together, drag a box around the diagram, come back, and press Control V.
    Look — the diagram sits under the question and above the options, exactly like the paper. The fraction options come from Sy Pad. Correct answer: B.

    Next to it, the reaction arrow from Sy Pad — ozone on top, zinc and water below.
    This one is numerical. Up in Question type, choose Numerical answer, click Exact value, and type the answer — one.
