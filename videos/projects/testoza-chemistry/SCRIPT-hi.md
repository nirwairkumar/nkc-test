# Script — Chemistry Equations in TestoZa (HI dub)

Hindi dub of SCRIPT-en.md for the YouTube Hindi audio track. The picture is the finished English
video, so nothing here moves the picture: capture/dub-hi.mjs places each line so every `{cue}` word
is spoken at or just after its on-screen action (same cue names as the English script). On-screen
labels stay in English exactly as they appear (Formula, Insert, Words, Match List, Picture…).
Chemical formulas are written as on screen; capture/hindi.mjs spells them out for the voice.
Lines are kept as long as the English window allows — shorten the Hindi rather than speed it up.
Never say "free" / "फ़्री" / "मुफ़्त" (owner rule).
