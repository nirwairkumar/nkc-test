We'll use real questions from JEE Main and JEE Advanced
2026.
Open Create Test, give it a name, pick JEE marking…
and straight to the questions.
First, a JEE Main question on complex ions.
The words, you just type.
For the formulas, open Maths and symbols
this is Sy Pad
and go to the Chemistry tab.
Now, the most useful button for any chemistry teacher
Formula.
Just type the formula the way you'd write it.
Bracket, M n, B r, four…
see?
The four drops down on its own.
For the charge, type a caret, then two minus.
Then the copper complex
six waters, two plus.
Insert, and Formula again for the two nickel complexes.
Every number drops down and every charge goes up,
while you type.
Insert…
and the whole line sits right in the question.
The options are plain text, and D is the right answer.
Next, an equilibrium.
Type the first line, then Formula, A,
the gas key, the reversible arrow, B…
and gas again.
The other keys work the same way: gas given off,
a precipitate, heat, degree Celsius,
aqueous, solid, liquid, K a, K b, pH and mol.
One tap each.
Insert.
Now the rest of the question
the concentrations, and what happens when we add more
A.
Then the four options, and D again.
Now, three reactions with conditions on the arrows.
Formula, sodium tetraborate…
then the arrow with a box above and below.
Here you choose how many lines go above and below.
We only need one, on top, so remove the bottom one,
and Insert.
That question mark above the arrow is your blank.
Tap delta for heat, step past the arrow with this key,
and finish the reaction.
Insert.
Second reaction: copper sulphate plus Y.
Same arrow
but now tap Words, and type non-luminous flame.
Words keep their spaces, like normal text.
Insert.
And the third one: two Z, two X and carbon on the left,
the arrow with luminous flame on top,
and two Q, borax and carbon monoxide on the right
exactly the same way.
Insert.
Type the question, the four options
plus two and plus two, and so on
and B is correct.
Match the list
JEE Main loves these.
In Sy Pad, open Tables, and choose Match List.
List-I and List-II are already there.
Type the labels
A, B, C, D and one to four
and Insert.
Every empty cell is now a blue box.
Click one, tap Formula, and type XeO3.
Next box
XeF2…
and so on, down both lists: XeO2F2,
XeOF4, then BrF5, NH3, the I3 minus ion,
and SF4.
Insert
and there it is: a clean two-column table,
right inside the question, just like the paper.
Type the four options
A two, B one, and so on
and B is the answer.
Now JEE Advanced, Paper 1.
The answer here is a number, so change the question type
to Numerical answer.
And JEE Advanced takes nothing off for a wrong number,
so set Wrong to zero.
You can set the marks on any question,
like this.
Type the question.
Now the first structure
tap Picture, then this cloud button.
It puts a picture inside the text, exactly where your
cursor is.
Choose a picture, find your file, pick the structure…
and there it is.
Now open Sy Pad, and right next to the structure,
the arrow with conditions.
Tap Words, type heat on top, add X,
and Insert.
The second structure goes in exactly the same way:
Picture, the cloud button, choose the file.
Then Sy Pad again
the arrow, the word heat on top, and Y.
Insert.
Just like the paper.
For the answer, choose Exact value,
and type four.
And when a question is long
don't type it at all.
More than one answer can be right here,
so first choose Multiple correct.
Now Fill from photo, choose a photo of the question…
and TestoZa reads it for you.
All three reactions, the gas labels,
the 573 kelvin, even U-235
and the photo shows the answer, so A and C are already
ticked.
Always give it one quick read.
Last thing
partial marks.
Choose JEE Advanced: one mark for each correct option
picked, full marks only for all of them.
Save test…
and your chemistry paper is in My Tests
ready to conduct as a live exam.
