# Script — How to Conduct a Cheat-Proof Online Exam (HI dub)

Hindi dub of SCRIPT-en.md for the YouTube Hindi audio track. The picture is the finished English
video, so nothing here moves the picture: capture/dub-hi.mjs places each line so every `{cue}` word
is spoken at or just after its on-screen action (same cue names as the English script; L23 adds the
WhatsApp highlight cues hname/htime/hlink/hinstr). On-screen labels stay in English exactly as they
appear (owner rule: on-screen text English, voice Hindi). Lines are kept as long as the English
window allows — shorten the Hindi rather than speed it up.
