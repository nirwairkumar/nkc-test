# TestoZa Short: PDF to online test (plan v1, 2026-10-15)

One idea carries the Short: **a stopwatch that never leaves the screen**. It starts at 0:00 when the
PDF goes in and stops at about 1:50 when a student is writing the test. It runs in real time except
while the AI reads, where it visibly fast-forwards (a "×5" badge), so the Short stays under a
minute without pretending the job takes less than it does. The real AI read in the source sample:
30 questions from a 6-page paper in 1:14.

Look: TestoZa's own light UI (the real screens, captured at 2–3× from the app's components) on a
soft night-blue stage, the same window carried through every frame (one world). Supers in short
two-tone English lines, upper third. Bottom 20% and the right edge kept clear for the Shorts UI.
Voice: Hindi, male; on-screen text English.
