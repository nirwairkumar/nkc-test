# Script — TestoZa: Understanding Results (EN)

One `## Lnn` block = one voice clip. Indented lines are spoken. `{cue}` marks the word an on-screen
action must land on (the click/appear moment is pinned just before the word after the cue).
Cues are stripped before text-to-speech. `[chapter]` lines are YouTube chapters.
Voice: ElevenLabs, adult male 28–30, Indian English, smooth and warm — a senior colleague showing you
his screen, not a presenter. Small human asides are written in; let them breathe.
Never say "free" (owner rule). **2D** = animated scene; **Screen** = real app take.

[chapter] 0:00 Sunday night, 48 answer sheets

## L01 · Cold open
**2D:** night desk under a lamp — a tall stack of answer sheets, a red pen, a cup of chai, steam gone.
**Delivery:** slow, warm, knowing. He has been there.

    {lamp}Sunday night. {stack}Forty-eight answer sheets from this morning's mock test. {pen}One red pen. {chai}And a cup of chai that went cold an hour ago.

## L02 · The weekend
**2D:** ticks and crosses flick across a sheet; totals; marks drop into a register; a rank list pinned to a notice board; calendar flips Sunday → Tuesday.

    {tick}You know how this goes. Tick, cross, tick… total. {register}Every mark into the register, {notice}then the rank list. {tuesday}And somewhere around Tuesday, it's finally done.

## L03 · The question nobody answers
**2D:** a question mark rises over the stack; a counter races to 960.
**Delivery:** a small laugh on the last line.

    {why}But the one question that really matters — which questions did my batch get wrong, and why? — {count}never gets answered. To know that, you'd have to count nine hundred and sixty answers by hand. {laugh}Nobody has that kind of Sunday.

## L04 · The turn
**2D:** the sheets fly into a laptop; the screen fills with numbers; title "Understanding Results".

    {online}On TestoZa, the checking is done the second they press Submit. {wall}But then you open the results, and it's a screen full of numbers. {title}So today, let's read them together. Both result pages, every number, and exactly what to do with them on Monday morning.

**2D:** one bar chart morphs through three shapes: piled right, piled left, two humps; then back to ours.

[chapter] 5:25 Teach these again — what the batch got wrong

**Screen:** the card on the right.

    {q13}Click Q13.
