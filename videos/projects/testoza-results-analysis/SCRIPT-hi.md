# Script — TestoZa: Understanding Results (HI dub)

Hindi audio track for the finished English video (YouTube multi-language audio). Same `## Lnn` ids
and `{cue}` names as SCRIPT-en.md: capture/dub.mjs places each line so every cue word is spoken just
after its on-screen action. Spoken Hinglish, the way coaching educators talk; app words stay
English, as they appear on screen. Same voice as English (ElevenLabs, Aarav J). Never say "free".

## L12 · Ranks and ties
