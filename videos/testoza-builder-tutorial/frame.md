# frame.md — TestoZa builder tutorial

Concept: a calm, expert "screen-side teacher" — the real builder is the hero; graphics only point,
label and pace. Same brand world as the launch film (videos/testoza-launch).

## Palette
- Stage: night blue `#020617` → `#050b1d`, cyan glow `rgba(14,116,144,.30)`, indigo glow `rgba(79,70,229,.22)`, faint 64px grid.
- Brand cyan `#38bdf8`, indigo `#818cf8`, gold Z gradient `#ffe885 → #f4b838 → #9e6400`.
- Text on dark `#f8fafc` / muted `#94a3b8`. Product UI keeps its own colours (recording).
- Pointer/highlight: cyan ring `#38bdf8` 3px with soft glow; key chips white on `#0f172a`.

## Type
- Outfit 600–900: titles, chapter numbers, logo.
- Mukta 400–800: all Hindi (Devanagari) and captions.

## Layout
- Recording sits in a floating browser window (rounded 18px, shadow) at 1760×990 on the stage; punch-ins scale the window, never the overlays.
- Chapter chip: top-left inside title-safe (x 192, y 108): number in a cyan circle + Hindi chapter name.
- Captions: bottom centre, Mukta 600 40px, white on `rgba(2,6,23,.78)` pill, max 2 lines, baseline above y 972 (title-safe).
- Key chips (Win + Shift + S, Ctrl + V): centred above captions.
- Cards (open, Part 2, close): full stage, centred type.
